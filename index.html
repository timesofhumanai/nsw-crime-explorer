Reading these numbers. These are incidents recorded by police, not a measure of how dangerous an area is. Larger suburbs record more simply because more people live and pass through them. Small suburbs swing year to year on a handful of events. A population-adjusted rate view is in development; until then, compare suburbs of similar size with care.
